Our Story

Driven by Purpose, Guided by Values

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization committed to creating lasting social impact. With its base in Chhattisgarh and work across India, the foundation brings together dedicated professionals and volunteers to respond to real community needs. Our work connects immediate relief with long-term change through education support, meals and clothing, animal care, women-led livelihood building, environmental action, and career development for young people.

Section 8 Registered

Registered as a non-profit company under Central Government guidelines.

80G & 12A Certified

Authorized to provide tax exemptions on contributions.

CSR-1 Registered

Registered with MCA to handle corporate social responsibility projects.

ISO 9001:2015

Certified international standards of quality management.

NGO Darpan - NITI Aayog Recognized

Officially listed and verified on the Government of India's NGO portal, ensuring total transparency and structural accountability.
